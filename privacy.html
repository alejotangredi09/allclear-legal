Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the All Clear AI Systems financial assistant uses, why we use it, and how we protect it. "All Clear AI Systems", "we", "us" and "our" mean the business that builds and runs the assistant. "Studio" means the tattoo studio that uses the service. "Owner" means the studio owner who talks with the assistant.

Short version
  • The assistant talks only with the studio owner, on WhatsApp.
  • It uses the studio's Clover sales, bank transactions and payments to artists to calculate what to pay each artist.
  • It never moves or sends money.
  • We do not sell data, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models.

1. What the service does

All Clear AI Systems provides a financial assistant for tattoo studios. Our first (pilot) client is Real Tattoo Company. The assistant:

  • reads the studio's sales and bank transactions every night;
  • sends the owner short WhatsApp messages when it needs an answer (for example, "Is this $100 payment a deposit?");
  • sends the owner a weekly summary with how much to pay each artist.

The studio's records and calculations are kept in a spreadsheet in the studio's own Google account.

2. WhatsApp: who the assistant talks to

  • The assistant uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (provided by Meta) through a separate business phone number.
  • It sends messages only to the studio owner's phone number, which the studio gives us during setup.
  • It only reads and acts on messages that come from that number. Messages from any other number are ignored.
  • It does not message artists, clients or anyone else, and it does not read the owner's other chats or contacts.
  • The owner can stop the WhatsApp messages at any time by telling us (see "Contact us"). The weekly email summary can continue as a backup if the studio wants.

3. Information we use

From WhatsApp

  • The owner's phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • The messages the owner sends to the assistant and the messages the assistant sends, with date, time and delivery status.

Sales from Clover (the studio's point-of-sale system)

  • Date, amount, tip and card surcharge of each payment.
  • Which employee or artist account charged the payment.
  • Clover payment IDs, so the same sale is never counted twice.

Bank transactions (read-only)

  • Date, amount and description of each transaction in the studio's business accounts, connected through Plaid with the studio's permission.
  • The name shown on transfers such as Zelle (for example, the client who paid or the artist who was paid).

Payments to artists and studio records

  • Artist names and their payment agreement with the studio (for example, a 60/40 split or a chair rental).
  • Amounts owed and paid to each artist, tips, deposits and expense categories.
  • For deposits: the appointment date and, if the owner gives it, the client's name.

What we do not collect

  • Full card numbers of the studio's clients. Clover does not share them with us.
  • Bank usernames or passwords. Bank login is handled by Plaid; we only receive read-only access.
  • Information from artists' or clients' phones.

4. How we use the information

  • To calculate how much the studio owes each artist each week.
  • To find payments that need a question (for example, unknown transfers or possible deposits) and ask the owner on WhatsApp.
  • To understand the owner's replies and record them in the studio's spreadsheet. The assistant always repeats the answer back to the owner for confirmation before it records anything.
  • To send the weekly summary and keep a clear record for the studio.
  • To fix errors and keep the service working.

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use the studio's data to train AI models.

5. Service providers we use

We share information only with the providers we need to run the service, and only for that purpose:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) – to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Twilio – to connect our system to WhatsApp.
  • Google (Sheets, Gmail, Apps Script) – to store the studio's records, run the calculations and send the email summary.
  • Clover – the source of the studio's sales data.
  • Plaid – read-only connection to the studio's bank accounts.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) – an AI service that helps the assistant understand the owner's written replies. We send only what is needed to understand the reply.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We may also share information if the law requires it, or with the studio's own accountant if the studio asks us to.

6. Who owns the data

The studio's financial data belongs to the studio. We use it only to provide the service to the studio.

7. How we protect it

  • Records are stored in the studio's own Google account, with access limited to the people who run the service.
  • Access keys for Clover, Plaid and other services are kept in protected settings, not in the spreadsheet or in messages.
  • Bank access is read-only. The assistant cannot make payments or transfers.

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information.

8. How long we keep it

  • We keep the information while the studio uses the service.
  • If the studio stops using the service, we delete the WhatsApp conversation history and remove our access within 30 days. The studio keeps its own spreadsheet and records.
  • We may keep some information longer only if the law requires it.

9. How to delete your data

To ask us to delete your data, email us at alejotangredi09@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the phone number you use with the assistant. We will:

  • confirm we received your request;
  • stop sending WhatsApp messages to that number;
  • delete the WhatsApp conversation history and any copies we hold within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.

You can also ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct it, at the same email address.

10. Children

The service is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
